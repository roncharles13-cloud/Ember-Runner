All on your phone — no network, no one else's data. It emits a faint ~18 kHz tone (most adults can't hear it; pets and kids sometimes can). Works best a few feet from a wall, in a not-too-noisy room. It senses motion, not identity. Needs mic permission and a secure (https) page. If breathing won't lock, sit still and close to the phone for ~20 s.

What this tier can honestly claim

This is RSSI tier — one scalar per sample from a laptop's own WiFi card. Presence and motion are reliable; breathing resolves only when a subject is close to the link and still, and low confidence is reported rather than hidden. No pose, silhouette, or through-wall — those need CSI hardware (ESP32 / Nexmon), which drops into sources/ without touching this pipeline. On a real laptop, python -m wifi_sense feeds the same charts from live WiFi; your effective fs will usually sit below 20 Hz depending on the driver.
